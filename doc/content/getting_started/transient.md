# Time-Dependent Simulation with Thermal-Hydraulic Coupling and Precursor Looping

The input files associated with this tutorial are found in [moltres/problems/2021-cnrs-benchmark](https://github.com/arfc/moltres/blob/devel/problems/2021-cnrs-benchmark). This benchmark is chosen for its simple demonstration of time-dependent MSR multiphysics.

The CNRS benchmark aims to facilitate code-to-code comparison of multiphysics simulations of molten salt reactors, particularly fast-spectrum MSRs.
The benchmark is split into three phases.

The results from the Moltres simulation of the CNRS benchmark are detailed in [Verification of Moltres for Multiphysics Simulations of Fast-Spectrum Molten Salt Reactors](http://arfc.github.io/papers/park_verification_2022.pdf).
